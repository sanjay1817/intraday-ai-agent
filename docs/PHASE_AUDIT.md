# Factual Implementation Audit — Phase-by-Phase

**Date:** 2026-07-13
**Method:** Every cell below was verified directly against the filesystem (`Read`/`Grep`/`wc -l`
on the actual files) and against this project's own phase specs recovered from the session
transcript — not against memory or assumption. Where the original per-file plan for a phase
could not be recovered verbatim, this is stated explicitly rather than guessed.

**Headline finding: 0 of 15 identifiable phases meet the COMPLETE bar defined below.**

## Completion rule (as specified)

A phase is COMPLETE only if all four hold:
1. Business logic exists (not just Pydantic contracts)
2. Unit tests exist and pass
3. Integration with dependent modules exists (something else in the codebase actually calls it)
4. It is actually executable (reachable/runnable, not just importable in isolation)

## Completion % methodology (disclosed, so the number isn't arbitrary)

`Completion % = 40%×(business logic built ÷ business logic planned) + 30%×(that logic's test
coverage) + 15%×(real, call-level integration — not just type reuse) + 15%×(executable end-to-end)`

## A note on phase numbering

Phases were never explicitly numbered "1" or "16" anywhere in this project's history. "Phase 2"
(Broker Integration) and "Phase 3" (Indicator Engine) are the first explicitly-numbered phases on
record; the unnumbered project foundation that preceded them is labeled **Phase 1** here purely by
inferred sequence. The unnumbered "Quant Research Platform" built after Phase 14 is labeled
**Phase 15** on the same basis. **No Phase 16 was ever specified in this project** — it is omitted
below rather than invented.

## Bottom line

- **0 of 15 real phases are COMPLETE** by the stated rule. The two most mature phases (Broker
  Integration, Indicator Engine) fail solely on **integration** (nothing in the codebase calls
  into them yet) — everything else fails on **business logic, tests, or both**.
- **11 of 15 phases are DTO/model-only** with zero business logic: AI Decision Engine, Market
  Data, Strategy, Risk, Paper Trading, Backtesting, Live Execution, Control Center, Multi-Agent
  AI, Portfolio Analytics — plus Platform, which has one real (but untested, unused) class.
- **Test coverage exists for exactly 2 of 15 phases** (Brokers, Indicators) — and even there, with
  material gaps (no WS reconnect tests, no retry-exhaustion tests, no edge-case tests).
- **The single most feature-complete phase (Research, Phase 15) has the least test protection of
  any non-trivial phase** — zero committed tests against ~25 functions of real numerical logic.
- Every phase's own plan (recovered from history) explicitly out-scoped its hardest work as
  "BLOCKED" or left it for later (Backtesting, Control Center) — this was a known, deliberate
  sequencing decision, not an oversight, but it means the dependency chain required to reach
  COMPLETE on any downstream phase is long: Backtesting alone is blocked on Phases 4, 6, 7, and 8
  simultaneously.
